# 首页 — Galileo AI Prompt

```
Design a homepage for "QUENTIN WINDOW" — a personal brand website for an independent developer named Quentin. The site serves as a content hub, product showcase, game center, and MVP validation platform.

Style: Bright, warm white, calm, refined, light tech, editorial, humanistic, indie developer vibe. NOT corporate SaaS.

Color palette: Warm white background (#FAFAF8), soft blue-grey accents (#B8C5D3), deep charcoal text (#2D2D2D), warm grey borders (#E8E6E1).

Typography: Clean sans-serif (Inter) for body, editorial-style headings with generous letter-spacing. Chinese text uses PingFang SC or Noto Sans SC.

Page sections from top to bottom:

1. Navigation: Fixed top bar with site logo "QUENTIN WINDOW" on the left. Nav links: 产品中心, 游戏中心, 实验室, 快来尝鲜, 关于. Clean, minimal. Transparent on hero, solid white on scroll.

2. Hero Section: Full-width warm white background. Left side: large headline "QUENTIN WINDOW" with subtitle "独立开发者的窗口 — 产品、内容与实验". Right side: collage-style visual with layered product screenshots, app icons, and decorative geometric shapes with subtle float animation. Two CTA buttons: "探索产品" (primary blue-grey) and "快来尝鲜" (secondary outline).

3. Product Matrix: Section title "核心产品" with subtle divider. 3 product cards side by side (stack on mobile): "个人助手" (personal productivity, published), "育儿助手" (parenting companion, beta), "AI工具助手" (AI tools collection, coming soon). Each card has product icon/illustration, product name, short description, status badge, and "了解更多" link. Card hover: lift up 4px with shadow expansion.

4. Quick Try Banner: Light blue-grey gradient background section. Headline "快来尝鲜" with tagline "抢先体验，支持独立开发". 1-2 featured offer cards with price and "立即尝鲜" CTA button. Subtle glow/highlight border on offer cards.

5. Dual IP Content Section: Two columns — "昆廷笔记" (left, tech/life notes) and "启鸣宝宝" (right, parenting). Each column has column name + icon, 2-3 article preview cards with cover image, title, summary (1 line), date. "进入专栏 →" link at bottom. Clean, editorial feel.

6. Games & Interactive Section: Title "游戏与互动". Game cards with playful but not childish design. Primary card: "拼音大冒险" with "在线试玩" button. Hover: slightly more vibrant (gentle scale + color shift).

7. Lab Preview: Title "实验室" with "探索中..." subtitle. 1-2 lab project cards with experiment status badges. Mysterious but inviting feel with slightly darker accent or gradient overlay. "进入实验室 →" link.

8. Community CTA: Clean section with centered text. "加入社区" headline with brief description. WeChat QR code placeholder + email link. Simple, trust-building layout.

9. Footer: Site name, copyright, navigation links organized in 4 columns: 产品, 内容, 关于, 联系. Social links / WeChat QR code. Clean, minimal.

Layout: Desktop full multi-column, max-width 1200px centered. Generous whitespace, section padding 80-120px vertically. Responsive: tablet 2-column, mobile single column with stacked cards.
```
