7. Footer: Standard site footer with 4-column navigation.

Content tone: First person ("我"), warm but professional, not boastful — just real. Chinese content with English section titles where appropriate.

Responsive: Desktop multi-column with large typography. Tablet 2-column. Mobile single column, reduced avatar size, timeline stays left-aligned.
