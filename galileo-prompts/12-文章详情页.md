# 文章详情页 — Galileo AI Prompt

```
Design an article detail page for "QUENTIN WINDOW" — a personal brand site. This is the reading experience for articles from two columns: "昆廷笔记" (tech/life) and "启鸣宝宝" (parenting). Optimized for comfortable long-form reading. Like a premium blog platform (Medium, Ghost) but warmer.

Color palette: Warm white background (#FAFAF8), deep charcoal text (#2D2D2D), dual accent: blue-grey (#B8C5D3) for Kunting articles, warm coral (#D4A89A) for Qiming articles, warm grey borders (#E8E6E1).

Typography: Optimized for reading — body text 16-18px, line-height 1.7-1.8, paragraph spacing 1.5em. Content width 720-800px for optimal reading.

Page sections:

1. Article Header: Breadcrumb "首页 > 昆廷笔记/启鸣宝宝 > 文章标题". Large article title (editorial, 28-36px). Cover image (full-width or contained, rounded corners). Meta row: author name · published date · view count · like count. Column badge: "昆廷笔记" (blue-grey) or "启鸣宝宝" (coral). Tags as clickable badges.

2. Table of Contents (Desktop Sidebar): Sticky sidebar on right (desktop only). Auto-generated from h2/h3 headings. Active section highlighted on scroll. Click to smooth-scroll. Mobile: collapsible TOC at top after header.

3. Article Body: Full-width content area (720-800px, centered). Rich text / Markdown rendered content. h2: 24px bold with top margin and subtle divider. h3: 20px semi-bold. Body: 16-18px line-height 1.75. Blockquotes: left border accent + italic + grey background. Code blocks: monospace, light grey background, rounded corners. Images: full-width with captions. Links: blue-grey accent, underline on hover. Images clickable to enlarge (lightbox).

4. Mid-Article Product Recommendation (at ~50% scroll): Visually distinct card breaking reading flow naturally. Related product mini-card: icon + name + one-line description + "了解详情 →" button. Subtle background (light blue-grey or warm tint). Not intrusive — feels like editorial insert.

5. End-of-Article Section: 互动区 — Like button with count + view count display. Heart/thumbs-up icon, click toggles with animation. 转化引导 — "了解更多产品" + "加入社区" CTA buttons.

6. Related Content: Title "相关推荐". 3-4 related article cards (same column or tags) + 1 related product card. Image + title + summary, click navigates.

7. Navigation: Previous/Next article links at bottom. "返回专栏" or "返回文章中心" link.

Reading experience priorities: Comfortable line-length (720-800px), generous line-height (1.75), clear heading hierarchy, subtle visual breaks, no distractions — content is king. Accent color adapts to column (blue-grey for Kunting, coral for Qiming).

Responsive: desktop sidebar TOC + centered content, tablet no sidebar with collapsible TOC at top, mobile single column reduced font with collapsed TOC.
```
