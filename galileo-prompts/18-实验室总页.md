Design a lab/experiments page for "QUENTIN WINDOW" — a personal brand site. Showcases experimental and in-development projects. Exploratory and forward-looking — a glimpse into the future. Like a research lab's project showcase.

6. Empty State: "实验室还在准备中..." with "去产品中心看看已发布的产品？" link.

Mood: Exploratory, forward-looking, intriguing. NOT mysterious to the point of confusion. Like peeking into a developer's workshop — exciting possibilities.

Max-width 1200px centered. Responsive: desktop 2-column with horizontal timeline, tablet 2-column, mobile single column with vertical timeline.
