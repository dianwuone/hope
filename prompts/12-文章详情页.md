# 文章详情页 v0.dev Prompt

## Prompt

```
Create an article detail page for "QUENTIN WINDOW". This is the reading experience for articles from two columns: "昆廷笔记" (tech/life) and "启鸣宝宝" (parenting). The page should be optimized for comfortable long-form reading.

## Design System

- **Color palette:** Warm white background (#FAFAF8), deep charcoal text (#2D2D2D), dual accent: blue-grey (#B8C5D3) for Kunting articles, warm coral (#D4A89A) for Qiming articles, warm grey borders (#E8E6E1).
- **Typography:** Optimized for reading — body text 16-18px, line-height 1.7-1.8, paragraph spacing 1.5em. Chinese: PingFang SC, Noto Sans SC.
- **Spacing:** Very generous line-height and paragraph spacing. Content width 720-800px for optimal reading.
- **Style:** Editorial, clean, content-focused. Like a premium blog platform (Medium, Ghost) but warmer.

## Page Structure

### 1. Article Header
- Breadcrumb: 首页 > 昆廷笔记/启鸣宝宝 > 文章标题
- Large article title (editorial, 28-36px)
- Cover image (full-width or contained, with rounded corners)
- Meta row: author name · published date · view count · like count
- Column badge: "昆廷笔记" (blue-grey) or "启鸣宝宝" (coral)
- Tags as clickable badges (navigate to tag aggregation page)

### 2. Table of Contents (Desktop Sidebar)
- Sticky sidebar on the right (desktop only)
- Auto-generated from h2/h3 headings in the article
- Active section highlighted based on scroll position
- Click to smooth-scroll to section
- On mobile: collapsible TOC at the top (after header)

### 3. Article Body
- Full-width content area (720-800px, centered)
- Rich text / Markdown rendered content
- Typography:
  - h2: 24px, bold, with top margin and subtle divider
  - h3: 20px, semi-bold
  - Body: 16-18px, line-height 1.75
  - Blockquotes: left border accent + italic + grey background
  - Code blocks: monospace, light grey background, rounded corners
  - Images: full-width within content, with captions
  - Links: blue-grey accent, underline on hover
- Images can be clicked to enlarge (lightbox)

### 4. Mid-Article Product Recommendation (inserted at ~50% scroll)
- A visually distinct card breaking the reading flow naturally
- Related product mini-card:
  - Product icon + name + one-line description
  - "了解详情 →" button
- Subtle background (light blue-grey or warm tint)
- Not intrusive — feels like a natural editorial insert
- Only appears if a related product exists

### 5. End-of-Article Section
- **互动区:** Like button (with count) + view count display
  - Like button: heart/thumbs-up icon, click toggles, animation on click
  - Already-liked state
- **转化引导:** "了解更多产品" + "加入社区" CTA buttons

### 6. Related Content
- Section title: "相关推荐"
- 3-4 related article cards (same column or same tags)
- 1 related product card (if applicable)
- Cards: image + title + summary, click navigates

### 7. Navigation
- Previous/Next article links at the bottom
- "返回专栏" or "返回文章中心" link

## Responsive
- Desktop: sidebar TOC + centered content
- Tablet: no sidebar, collapsible TOC at top
- Mobile: single column, reduced font size, TOC collapsed

## Interactions
- TOC: smooth scroll, active section highlight
- Like button: click animation (heart fills, count increments)
- Images: click to enlarge (lightbox overlay)
- Product recommendation: subtle appear animation
- Related articles: hover lift
- Breadcrumb: hover underline

## Reading Experience Priorities
- Comfortable line-length (720-800px)
- Generous line-height (1.75)
- Clear heading hierarchy
- Subtle visual breaks between sections
- No distractions — content is king
- Accent color adapts to column (blue-grey for Kunting, coral for Qiming)

## Tech Stack
- Next.js App Router
- Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion for scroll animations
- shadcn/ui for buttons, badges
- MDX or react-markdown for content rendering
- Intersection Observer for TOC highlighting
```
